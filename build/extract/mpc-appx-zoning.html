Appendix: Zoning districts

Zoning designation Uses Zoning district categories

Additional bulk and density designations (e.g. M1-1, M2-3) correspond to more specific requirements. As the bulk and density number increases, the zoning code allows bigger buildings. For residential districts, instead of dashes, the bulk and density number is contained in the district name (e.g. RS2, RT4).

Residential (R) Residential uses (housing) as well as some public and civic uses (such as libraries, fire stations, and schools).
  • RS (Residential Single-Unit): Detached houses on individual lots, and limited two-flats
  • RT (Residential Two-Flat, Townhouse and Multi-Unit): Townhouses and low-density multi-unit residential
  • RM (Residential Multi-Unit): Multi-unit residential buildings with moderate to high density

Areas that are part of a Planned Development are included in this category, rather than their underlying zoning district(s).
Manufacturing (M) Manufacturing, warehousing, wholesale, and industrial uses (such as factories, grocery wholesalers, and waste disposal facilities).
  • M1 (Limited Manufacturing/Business Park): Low-impact manufacturing, wholesaling, warehousing and distribution within enclosed buildings
  • M2 (Light Industry): Moderate-impact manufacturing, wholesaling, warehousing and distribution, including storage and work-related activities outside of enclosed buildings; more land-intensive activities
  • M3 (Heavy Industry): High-impact manufacturing and industrial uses, including extractive and waste-related uses
 11%
Parks and Open Space (POS) Public open space, public parks, and public beaches; also includes cemeteries.
  • POS-1 (Regional or Community Park)
  • POS-2 (Neighborhood Park, Mini-Park or Playlot)
  • POS-3 (Open Space or Natural Area)
  • POS-4 (Cemetery)
 8%
Planned Manufacturing District (PMD) Each PMD has its own individualized set of allowed uses, often industrial uses and some civic and commercial uses. PMDs do not allow residential uses. Each PMD is its own district with individualized set of uses, generally used for industrial corridors. 6%
Business (B) Retail and service uses (such as cafes and dry cleaners), some public and civic uses, and some residential uses.
  • B1 (Neighborhood Shopping): Small scale retail and service uses with a pedestrian orientation, permits residential above the ground floor
  • B2 (Neighborhood Mixed-Use): Same as B1, but allows ground-floor residential uses
  • B3 (Community Shopping): Shopping centers, larger buildings, destination oriented for cars
 6%
Commercial (C) Retail and services uses, and more intensive commercial uses and tend to be larger and more oriented to users in cars (such as banks and car washes), with some residential, public and civic uses, plus manufacturing allowed in some districts.
  • C1 (Neighborhood Commercial): Similar to B3, plus more intensive, car-oriented uses
  • C2 (Motor Vehicle-Related Commercial): High intensity business or commercial, allowing virtually any type of business, service, or commercial use
  • C3 (Commercial, Manufacturing and Employment): High intensity commercial use intended to serve as a buffer to manufacturing districts; does not permit residential uses
 3%
Downtown (D) Only in the designated “downtown” area; a variety of uses with high density, including public, civic, commercial, and residential.
  • DC (Downtown Core): Allows a broad mix of office, commercial, public, recreation, and entertainment uses, along with mixed-use and residential
  • DX (Downtown Mixed-Use): Allows office, commercial, public, institutional, and residential with vertical mixed-uses that contain active ground floors
  • DR (Downtown Residential): Allows residential and small-scale commercial uses on lower floors, with residential above
  • DS (Downtown Service): Allows a mix of small-scale office, commercial services, public use, transportation and communication services, and industrial uses
 1%
Transportation (T) Uses related to transportation.
  • T (Transportation): Land used to protect roads, rail, busways, pedestrian and bicycle trails, and minor utilities.
 <1%
