  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}Strong transit-connected business corridors provide employment and wealth-building opportunities, neighborhood amenities, and 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}walkable shared space. But MPC research shows that business and commercial activity—measured through business concentration 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}and vacancy rates—is inequitably distributed across Chicago. Although zoning is not necessarily the exclusive cause of these 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}outcomes, our analysis identified inequities in the distribution of pedestrian-oriented zoning designations and zoning for car-
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}dominated businesses—highlighting the opportunity to take a citywide approach to encouraging walkable, vibrant pedestrian-
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}oriented business corridors. These corridors can be cornerstones of economic growth, tools to reduce vacancies, and landing 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}places for small businesses.

[B 58,553,340,698]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}When {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #0000ff}Columbus, OH{ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000} began updating its 70-year-old zoning code, it 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}was clear that building more housing alone wouldn’t create vibrant 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}communities. Columbus’s “Zone In” plan, adopted by its City Council 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}in 2024, approached development holistically, establishing mixed-
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}use districts citywide. 140 miles of key corridors across the whole city 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}will allow 88,000 more units of housing alongside more spaces for 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}businesses and essential services. With different zoning allowances 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}across six types of mixed-use districts, Columbus created more flexibility 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}to build complete communities: more homes supporting affordability, 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}more customers for local businesses, more jobs and lively streets, 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}anchored around transit that allows people to save time and money 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}when moving around the city.
