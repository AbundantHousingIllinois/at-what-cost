  {ProximaNova-Regular 15.0 #4e5154}What is the Zoning and Land Use Assessment? 
[B 49,114,566,163]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}Reimagining a new citywide status quo for zoning in Chicago requires a shared understanding of the effects our current zoning and 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}land use policies are having on neighborhoods across the city. That’s why MPC—with research support from the Urban Institute—
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}created the Zoning and Land Use Assessment: a collaborative research and engagement initiative designed to explore the impacts 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}of Chicago’s current zoning and land use policies, and propose improvements to better serve public health, equity, and sustainability. 
[B 49,176,426,197]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 15.0 #4e5154}Who contributed to the Zoning and Land Use Assessment? 
[B 49,203,558,252]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}The Assessment was a multi-year project that engaged hundreds of stakeholders. Since the project’s launch in 2022, MPC has 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}collaborated with dozens of partners from civic and community groups, government, and the private sector. This engagement took 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}many forms, including one-on-one conversations, focus groups, advisory committees, and public events. Two advisory groups—the 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}Steering Committee and Community Advisory Group—in particular provided essential guidance for the Assessment.
