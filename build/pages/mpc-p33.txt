  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}competing priorities; guide the development of vibrant, walkable business corridors across the city; buffer high-impact industry from

  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}planning be durable and long-term enough to extend beyond individual mayoral administrations; tied to mandated corresponding 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}land use updates; and reliably budgeted and staffed by the City.

  {ProximaNova-Bold 9.0 #000000}PUBLIC ENGAGEMENT 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}In 2007, the Minneapolis City Council adopted the Core Principles 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}of Community Engagement from The International Association 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}of Public Participation. These principles were expanded in 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}the “Blueprint for Equitable Engagement” in 2015. The City of 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}Minneapolis’ Neighborhood and Community Relations Department 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}follows this blueprint department-wide, and it was used to facilitate 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}meaningful public engagement in the 2040 Comprehensive Plan.
