  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}We assumed bidirectional relationships between healthcare access and zoning: The amount of power healthcare 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}clinics might have in negotiations with local planning offices is not measurable, and thus it’s not possible to 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}identify whether zoning created healthcare siting or whether healthcare siting desires paved the way for 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}the zoning to be accessible. That is why we restricted the analysis methods to correlations and simple OLS 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}regressions without attempting longitudinal analyses with more complex causal inference mechanisms because it 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}could lead to spurious conclusions that attribute too much influence to zoning when it was in fact negotiated/the 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}other way around.

  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}One of the most important limitations in these research findings is that zoning has little influence over healthcare 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}clinic siting (since these appeared to have few restrictions from the zoning code and it seemed likely they 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}would be determined by the amount of money their patients could offer via insurance enrollment rates) and 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}instead zoning’s influence on healthcare clinic siting appears to be mostly mediated by its influence on housing 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}affordability/income distributions across the city. The largest limitation of this research is that it was conducted 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}at the tract level rather than the point level, and thus it’s nigh impossible to directly associate the location of 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}healthcare clinics with distinctive zones. Additionally, the available data on counts and density of “physician 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}clinics” is a poor identifier of the key outcome as it doesn’t standardize an operational definition for what counts 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}as access, but it was the best available data source.
