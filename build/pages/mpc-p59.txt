  {ProximaNova-Bold 11.0 #000000}Documenting and synthesizing feedback on recommendations
[B 49,243,549,279]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}In workshops between 2024 and 2025, MPC collected more than 1,200 pieces of feedback from stakeholders on an initial set 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}of zoning reform recommendations, which were used to update the recommendations to become the revised set that eventually 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}appeared in this report. 
[B 49,288,552,348]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}In this process, MPC documented each individual piece of feedback received—whether through interaction on an activity board, 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}notes in a workshop workbook, or other feedback channels—in a central spreadsheet for each of the project’s priority outcomes 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}(with the exception of Productive Land Use, which did not have a set of proposed recommendations). Alongside this qualitative 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}feedback on the draft recommendations, voting was used to gauge levels of agreement among stakeholders with each individual 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}recommendation, and votes were also individually tracked and summarized.
[B 49,357,561,393]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}MPC staff reviewed and coded the feedback to summarize the themes and issues that emerged most frequently, in these 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}categories: (1) This could be effective because (2) This could be improved by (3) If this was implemented, things to consider (4) What 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}is missing. These themes were used by teams of MPC staff, alongside the voting results, to update recommendations.
