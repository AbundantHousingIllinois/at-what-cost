  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}In {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #0000ff}RESEARCH FINDINGS AND POLICY RECOMMENDATIONS{ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}, you’ll find a deep dive into MPC’s recommended policies for 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}improving zoning and land use in Chicago. The recommendations, which were developed in consultation with stakeholders, are 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}organized by the challenges they respond to: the worsening housing affordability crisis in Chicago, the inequitable distribution of 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}jobs and business vitality, exposure to pollutants and climate vulnerabilities, an opaque and difficult-to-navigate zoning system, and 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}piecemeal planning practices. In each category, you’ll find the research findings from the Assessment that relate to the challenge, 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}MPC’s specific policy recommendations, additional considerations for policy design and implementation, and example policies from 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}other cities.
