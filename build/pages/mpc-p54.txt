  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}A major limitation was with the flooding data we had access to. Presently, there is still no data source that 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}accurately captures flooding occurrences at a granular level. For instance, CMAP’s urban flood susceptibility 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}index is a probabilistic index. The City’s 311 flood complaints are somewhat biased toward single family residential 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}neighborhoods. In our analysis, we combined the two data sources to come up with a more robust index for 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}flooding. Another major limitation is that urban heat and flooding are individually complex and are influenced by 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}many different factors that we could not account for in our research. For instance, with regard to urban flooding, 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}we primarily focused on above-ground risk factors such as impervious surfaces and green infrastructure, despite 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}the importance of below-ground infrastructure, such as the City’s sewer system.
