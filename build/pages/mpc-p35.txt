[B 49,222,564,295]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}This outcome examined the relationship between infrastructure and density citywide. To understand infrastructure access, MPC 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}developed an Urban Services Index, which combined measures to capture the quality of and the proximity to infrastructure 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}across three categories: Transportation, Education, and Neighborhood Amenities. MPC also developed a Density Index based on 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}population density, job density, and the amount of building space compared to developable land. These indices were calculated 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}and assigned to block groups, which were then ranked by quartile. The rankings were used to identify different typologies related to 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}infrastructure access and density (e. g. Low Infrastructure, Moderate Density).

  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}While disparities exist in public services across neighborhoods, 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}every part of the city has strengths to support quality of life and 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}continued growth. Using our Urban Services Index, MPC’s 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}analysis indicated that the Northwest and Southwest sides 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}showed strong scores on education infrastructure, the Central 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}and West sides strong scores on transportation infrastructure, 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}and the North and Southeast strong scores on neighborhood 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}amenities. Top-scoring neighborhoods citywide for infrastructure 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}access and quality include The Loop, Edgewater, Uptown, 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}Kenwood, Near South Side, and Hyde Park.
