  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}To create space for more housing, MPC recommends 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}legalizing building types of up to at least four-flats on 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}residential land across the city and updating accompanying 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}bulk and density requirements to allow these types of 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}construction. MPC also recommends legalizing additional 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}dwelling units (ADUs) on all residential land. ADUs are 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}additional homes connected to or outside an existing 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}building (like a basement or attic apartment, or a coach 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}house). Chicago’s City Council expanded ADU allowances 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}in 2025,{ProximaNova-Regular 5.2 #000000}12{ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000} but in the ongoing tradition of aldermanic 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}prerogative, this expansion requires alders to opt-in any 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}area zoned RS in their wards, rather than allowing ADUs 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}by-right citywide.
