  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}“We want to see access to nature in our 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}neighborhoods—trees and plants, an open, 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}public riverfront, parks, and healthy air. We 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}want to walk down safe and accessible 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}streets that are friendly for pedestrians, 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}bicyclists, children, and people of all ages 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}and abilities. We want the places we live 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}and work to feel welcoming and active, 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}with affordable housing options that 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}prevent displacement, do not compete with 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}sustainability needs, and neighborhoods 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}of a sufficient density to avoid sprawl. We 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}want consistent and reliable transit access, 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}fresh food and clean water, cultural events, 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}schools, and a variety of small businesses 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}that serve the community. We aspire to have 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}people of all ages spending time out and 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}about, in clean spaces filled with uses that 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}meet community needs. Finally, we envision 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}a community that is resilient and adaptive 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}to changing climate, social, and economic 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 12.0 #4e5154}conditions.”
