  {IvyPrestoHeadline-Regula 20.0 #e36841}What is land use? What is zoning?
[B 49,82,563,119]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}Land use is how Chicagoans experience the places we live, work, and visit in our daily lives. The apartment building next door, the 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}nearby park, that favorite corner store or local coffee shop, that empty lot or big-box store, the factory or car wash across the street—
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}all are examples of different land uses.

[IMAGE 97,572,215,690]
[B 281,127,563,212]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}Zoning regulates land uses by setting limits on what can be built, and 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}where. In this report, “zoning” includes the zoning ordinance (also 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}known as the zoning code or text) which sets out rules for development 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}in different zoning districts, the zoning map which determines the 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}districts that apply to different areas of the city, and the zoning process—
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}the people and pathways through which decisions about zoning get 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}made. 
[B 281,220,565,305]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}Together, these things determine which land uses are allowed in specific 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}locations and set limits on new development’s size and shape. Zoning 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}controls how tall a building can be and how close it can be to the street. 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}It influences allowable density: how many of something, like homes 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}or storefronts, exist in a space. Zoning also influences how long the 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}development process takes, how much it costs, and who can weigh in 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}along the way.
[B 281,313,556,398]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}Zoning reform isn’t a silver bullet. Many other factors also play an 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}important role in shaping development and the feel of neighborhoods. 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}This encompasses everything from other regulatory systems like 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}building codes and business licensing, to the market for development 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}(including what financing is available for development), taxes and 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}other revenue structures, and community institutions that anchor 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}neighborhoods.

[B 281,456,447,477]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 15.0 #4e5154}Why does zoning matter? 
[B 281,483,558,568]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}Improving zoning means improving life for Chicagoans. Zoning is 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}one of the most direct tools policymakers have to influence how 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}neighborhoods look and feel. As the research in this report shows, our 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}current zoning system does not always result in equitable outcomes. 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}Zoning is part of a long, ongoing history of laws and practices that 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}have contributed to a segregated Chicago, with disinvestment in some 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}neighborhoods and abundant resources in others. 
[B 281,576,557,673]
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}Currently, zoning is controlled through a longstanding practice called 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}“aldermanic prerogative.” Alderpersons have control over what gets 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}built within the boundaries of each of our 50 wards. This approach has 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}not been successful in meeting our challenges: Chicago is seen as 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}too unpredictable a place to build, housing continues to become more 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}expensive, and segregation continues to divide our city. We have an 
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}opportunity with zoning to think as a city, moving beyond a ward-by-
  {ProximaNova-Regular 9.0 #000000}ward system and taking a bigger-picture policy approach.
